Workflows

Workflows are used to automate small processes within their environment. They enable the replication and complete removal of repetitive tasks.

  • Triggers

    They are the root of every workflow. This is because the triggers set the workflow in motion and initiate the chain of actions or conditions. The list of all possible triggers appears when you specify the trigger in the workflow menu.

  • Action

    The actions are the actual heart of every workflow. They fulfill a task selected by you when the workflow is triggered. The order in which the actions are executed is sequential and can be freely selected by you.

  • Condition

    Conditions can also be integrated into the workflow as required. The conditions control the sequential process and can, for example, interrupt the further execution of actions

Processes

Now that you know what a workflow is, we ask you to take a step backwards. Because when workflows automate certain processes, in many cases it is an interplay of workflows that enables the most efficient automation. This is exactly what processes are used for. Each process is a sequence of so-called "steps". Each step has the ability to execute a workflow.
